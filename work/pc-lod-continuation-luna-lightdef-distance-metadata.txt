## New high-relevance LightDef metadata

A contiguous editor/schema string cluster identifies `LightDef` at RVA `0x47ECB68`, with the following member names at `0x47ED470..0x47ED6D0`: `AttenuationRadius`, `CutoffRadius`, `CutOnDist`, `ShadowCutOnDist`, `FadeOutDist`, `NearFadeClipDist`, `NearFadeStartDist`, `ShadowFadeOutDist`, and `SpecFadeOutDist`.

The associated descriptions at RVAs `0x47EE0B0`, `0x47EF6F0`, `0x47EF780`, `0x47EF860`, `0x47EF910`, and `0x47EF980` document:

- `AttenuationRadius`: a theoretical bounding sphere radius after which the light cuts off; this further attenuates physical falloff.
- `FadeOutDist`: the distance to the front of the light's bounding sphere when it fades out and is then culled/not drawn.
- `NearFadeClipDist`: the distance from the camera at which the light center's intensity is fully faded to zero; negative values can dim the light when close.
- `NearFadeStartDist`: the camera distance from the light center where intensity begins fading toward zero; zero disables near fading.
- `ShadowFadeOutDist`: after this distance, shadows fade out and stop being evaluated.
- `SpecFadeOutDist`: after this distance, the light becomes diffuse-only.

The first four behaviors are materially different. In particular, `NearFade*` explicitly use light-center-to-camera distance for intensity, while `FadeOutDist` describes a camera-distance fade/cull of the light as a draw object. Those per-light properties are a much closer match to the report that nearby surfaces stop receiving local-light illumination as the Photo camera moves away than `VehicleSystemConfig.MaxCulledLightDistance` or shadow LOD.

## What is still unproven

This is authoring/schema metadata, not a demonstrated PC renderer consumer. The exact strings have no direct RIP-relative code references in the existing index, and this scan did not locate an upload function, runtime light structure, active Corson V authored values, or the code computing the camera distance. The executable does not contain MSVC RTTI descriptors for `LightDef`, `LevelLightDef`, `LightComponent`, `PointLight`, `OmniLight`, or `LightingView`; the previously found `LevelLightManager` vtable is generic descriptor/registration work. Thus this establishes intended semantics for authored fields, but not whether the specific Corson V lights use those fields on PC or whether their camera origin is the Photo render view.

The separate strings `DistantLightingGround/Mid/Roof/ForceEmbedded` and `BypassLightGrids` refer to distant-lighting/light-grid behavior and do not establish a local-light cull path. Do not globally alter them to address point/spot light falloff.
